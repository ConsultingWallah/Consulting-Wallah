Who is the Academy for?

Final-year students and fresh graduates (B.Pharm, Pharm.D, BBA, MBA, hospital administration, engineering, nursing and allied health), early professionals with up to five years' experience, and teams in hospitals, distributors and pharma companies.

Who teaches?

Practitioners who run procurement, pharma category management and analytics inside multi-hospital groups today, plus invited industry experts for specialist sessions.

Are the courses online?

Yes — live online in Gulf and India time, recorded for replay. Corporate programmes can be delivered on site in the UAE and India.

What does it cost?

Fees are shared with the cohort details after you apply; scholarships are available for a limited number of students per cohort. Mentoring sessions and corporate programmes are quoted separately.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes — a Consulting Wallah certificate plus a portfolio project you can show an employer. Strong participants are considered for the internship-cum-training programme.

Is there anything free?

Yes: the fortnightly newsletter, the Drug and Device Explorers, the catalogue benchmarks, templates and report summaries — the starter kit on this page.
