Questions

Are the templates really free?

Yes. Free to use and share with attribution. No sign‑up is required for the templates; look‑ups may ask for an email so we can send updates.

Can I use them outside healthcare?

Yes. The spend cube and tender templates work for any organisation that buys from suppliers; the Drug Explorer and alternatives repository are healthcare‑specific.

Where does the Explorer data come from?

Public registers only: DoH Abu Dhabi Shafafiya, FDA NDC and Orange Book, NPPA and Jan Aushadhi, EMA, WHO ATC/DDD. Updated monthly. Verify with the regulator before purchase or dispensing.

Can you run this on our real data?

Yes — the free executive assessment for hospital leaders does exactly that, four slots a month.
