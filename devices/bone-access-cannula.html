How many bone access cannula products are registered in the US?

411 device records in commercial distribution are listed on the FDA Global Unique Device Identification Database (GUDID) under the GMDN category "Bone access cannula", from 36 companies. The largest labeler is Ranfac Corp. with 35% of records.

Which FDA product codes apply?

KNW (Instrument, Biopsy), NBH (Accessories, arthroscopic), PDQ (Neurosurgical Nerve Locator), GAA (NEEDLE, ASPIRATION AND INJECTION, DISPOSABLE)

Is this the same as UAE/GCC registration?

No. GUDID is the US register. For the UAE (MOHAP/DoH) and Saudi (SFDA/MDMA) a separate registration and a local authorised representative are required; Consulting Wallah can check the status and connect you with registered suppliers.
