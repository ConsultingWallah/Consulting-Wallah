How to use it

Hospital and pharmacy buyers

Check every registered brand and its public and pharmacy price before a tender. The price spread table on each molecule shows the gap between the cheapest and dearest registered brand at the same strength — usually the saving a class-level lot can capture.

Manufacturers and exporters

See who already holds your molecule in the UAE, which agents represent your competitors, and whether the US and EU registers show generic entry. Then use the agent directory to shortlist partners.

Distributors and investors

Portfolio pages for all 480 agents: packs, molecules, manufacturers represented and therapy areas — a map of the UAE distribution landscape.

Float a requirement — get comparable quotes

Hospitals, pharmacies and distributors: tell us what you need and the Consulting Wallah Network returns comparable quotes from verified suppliers in the UAE, India and beyond. No fee for buyers.
