Executive summary

Self-sufficient generics industry preparing for the end of the LDC patent waiver. Published estimates normalised to 2025 range from USD 3.2 bn to USD 6.0 bn; the Consulting Wallah central estimate is USD 4.0 bn, growing at about 11% a year to USD 8.3 bn by 2032. Scope differences between publishers explain most of the spread — the comparison table below states each one.

Growth is driven by local manufacturers meet ~98% of domestic demand, up from under 10% in the early 1980s (TBS, Daily Star, BIDA); exports growing: USD 238.5 mn in FY2025-26 (+12%) and +40% in Q1 FY2026-27 (bdnews24); lDC TRIPS pharma waiver has allowed production of ~20% patented molecules without licence costs (Daily Star).

Context that matters for buyers and suppliers: Local companies meet about 98% of Bangladesh's medicine demand. 213 active pharmaceutical companies (BIDA); BAPI has over 150 member companies (TBS). Industry employs ~200,000 directly and ~300,000 indirectly. Bangladesh imports over 85% of APIs at ~USD 1.3 bn per year (BIDA); other sources say 97% of APIs imported.

Key facts and context

  • Local companies meet about 98% of Bangladesh's medicine demand (BIDA; Daily Star; bdnews24)
  • 213 active pharmaceutical companies (BIDA); BAPI has over 150 member companies (TBS) (BIDA; TBS)
  • Industry employs ~200,000 directly and ~300,000 indirectly (bdnews24)
  • Bangladesh imports over 85% of APIs at ~USD 1.3 bn per year (BIDA); other sources say 97% of APIs imported (BIDA; TBS)
  • Pharma exports FY2025-26: USD 238.5 mn, ~5.5x the FY2010-11 level of USD 44.2 mn (bdnews24 / EPB)
  • About 20% of drugs made in Bangladesh are still under patent elsewhere; 80% are off-patent (The Daily Star (CPD estimate))
  • API Industrial Park: 200 acres at Munshiganj (Gazaria) under BSCIC, announced 2008; 4 of 42 plots operating by Aug 2025 (BIDA; Daily Star)
  • 1982 National Drug Policy banned over 1,700 'irrational' formulations; in the early post-1971 period eight MNCs held over 70% of the market (The Daily Star)
  • Beximco, Square and Incepta accounted for nearly half of pharma exports last year (bdnews24)
  • Exports began in 1985; Gonoshasthaya Pharmaceuticals began API (amoxicillin) production in 1988 (bdnews24)

GCC and India outlook

Where the GCC and India sit

  • Bangladesh domestic market: USD 3.5 bn+ (2025) — The Daily Star
  • Bangladesh pharma exports: USD 238.5 mn (FY2025-26) — bdnews24 / EPB
  • Bangladesh annual API imports: USD 1.2 bn (Daily Star) / USD 1.3 bn (BIDA) (2025) — The Daily Star; BIDA
  • Export destinations: 150+ countries (BIDA, TBS); 'over 160 countries' (Daily Star) (2025) — BIDA; Daily Star

Consulting Wallah works this market from both sides: hospital groups in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, India and Bangladesh as buyers, and manufacturers in India, China, Singapore, Malaysia and the US as suppliers through the Network. Our observation is that registration status, in-country stock and structured quotations decide access in the GCC, while price and distributor reach decide it in India. CW observation

Opportunities we see: industry target of USD 500 mn export earnings by next fiscal year; 1% of a ~USD 400 bn global generics market would add ~USD 4 bn (BAPI, bdnews24); tBS cites targets of USD 450 mn exports in 2025 and USD 1.5 bn by 2030; aPI localisation: 200-acre BSCIC API park at Munshiganj with tax holidays to 2032 (BIDA).

Market dynamics

Growth drivers

  • Local manufacturers meet ~98% of domestic demand, up from under 10% in the early 1980s (TBS, Daily Star, BIDA)
  • Exports growing: USD 238.5 mn in FY2025-26 (+12%) and +40% in Q1 FY2026-27 (bdnews24)
  • LDC TRIPS pharma waiver has allowed production of ~20% patented molecules without licence costs (Daily Star)
  • Fiscal incentives: 100% tax holiday for 5 API molecules and 7.5% for 3 molecules to 2032; 6% export cash subsidy on pharma products and 5% on APIs (BIDA)
  • Domestic market roughly tripled from Tk 90 bn (2010) to Tk 300 bn+ (bdnews24)

Restraints

  • ~85-97% of APIs are imported (BIDA: over 85%, ~USD 1.3 bn/yr; TBS: 97%; Daily Star: ~USD 1.2 bn/yr)
  • LDC graduation scheduled 24 Nov 2026 ends TRIPS pharma exemption; Beximco COO warned drug prices 'could go up tenfold or more' for patented molecules (Daily Star)
  • API Industrial Park at Gazaria/Munshiganj announced 2008; only 4 of 42 plots operational as of Aug 2025 (Daily Star)
  • Medicines are only ~0.5% of merchandise exports vs ~USD 48 bn total exports (bdnews24)

Opportunities

  • Industry target of USD 500 mn export earnings by next fiscal year; 1% of a ~USD 400 bn global generics market would add ~USD 4 bn (BAPI, bdnews24)
  • TBS cites targets of USD 450 mn exports in 2025 and USD 1.5 bn by 2030
  • API localisation: 200-acre BSCIC API park at Munshiganj with tax holidays to 2032 (BIDA)
  • 1,200 products registered for export over the past two years (bdnews24)
  • Government applied for a three-year extension of LDC status / TRIPS waiver extension requested to 2029 or 2032-33 (bdnews24, Daily Star, TBS)

Challenges

  • Post-graduation patent/licence costs and loss of duty-free market access from 2026-2029
  • Heavy import dependence on India and China for raw materials
  • Only Beximco, Square and Incepta account for nearly half of exports - concentrated export base (bdnews24)
  • Few international regulatory approvals constrain entry into regulated markets
  • Insulin prices could rise eightfold without TRIPS exemption (South Centre study via Daily Star)

What it means for you

  • Manufacturers: registration and a strong local agent decide access; serialisation and GMP inspection readiness shorten the path; localisation stories win public tenders.
  • Hospital leaders: class-level lots, biosimilars and IV→oral switch programmes are the three savings levers; the drug repository lists the class members.
  • Distributors: buyers increasingly want data (catalogue, serialisation events, fill rates) as much as product.
