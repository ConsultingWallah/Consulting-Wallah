Executive summary

India’s pharmaceutical industry is the world’s largest supplier of generic medicines by volume and a fast-growing domestic market in its own right. Published estimates for the 2025 domestic market range from USD 28.0 bn to USD 60.0 bn; our central estimate is USD 28.0 bn, growing at about 8% to USD 48.0 bn by 2032. Exports are of a similar order of magnitude and growing, led by the US, Africa, the UK and increasingly the GCC.

The domestic market is a branded-generics market: thousands of brands of the same molecules, sold through a dense distribution chain, with price control on NLEM molecules (DPCO) and the Jan Aushadhi scheme pushing unbranded generics. For hospital buyers this means large price dispersion for the same molecule — and large savings from tendering by molecule and class rather than by brand.

Key facts and context

  • India ranks 3rd globally by pharma volume and 11th by value; ~3,000 companies and ~10,500 manufacturing units; ~500 API makers (~8% of global API industry) (PIB)
  • India supplies ~20% of global generic medicines; ~60,000 generic brands across 60 therapeutic categories (PIB)
  • Exports USD 30.47 bn in FY2024-25 (16x the USD 1.9 bn of 2000-01), to 191 countries, ~50% to highly regulated markets (PIB)
  • Jan Aushadhi: 18,646+ kendras (Feb 2026), 2,110 medicines + 315 devices; sales Rs 2,022.47 crore FY25 (citizen savings ~Rs 8,000 crore); Rs 1,409.32 crore Apr-Nov FY26 (PIB)
  • Pharma PLI (since 2021): Rs 40,890 crore investment; sales Rs 3,16,797 crore incl. Rs 2,03,730 crore exports; ~97,000 jobs (to Sep 2025) (PIB)
  • Bulk drug PLI: 55,100 MT/yr capacity for 26 critical APIs/KSMs; sales Rs 2,313 crore; import avoidance Rs 3,591 crore (PIB)
  • GCC = 5.58% of Indian pharma exports; WANA exports USD 1,749.68 mn in FY25 vs USD 1,320.44 mn in FY21 (Medical Dialogues / Pharmexcil)
  • US takes 34% and Europe 19% of India's pharma exports; >60% go to highly regulated markets (Pharmexcil, Apr 2026) (Chemindigest)
  • Domestic IPM 2025: Rs 2,40,672 crore (+8.1%); 2026 forecast +7.8-8.1%; Augmentin #1 and Glycomet GP #2 on MAT basis; Mounjaro #1 in Dec 2025 (Pharmarack via Tribune)

GCC and India outlook

India

Chronic therapies (cardiac, diabetes, respiratory, CNS) are the growth engine; hospital channel is consolidating with chains; trade generics and e-pharmacy are reshaping retail. Biosimilars (Biocon, Dr Reddy’s, Intas, Zydus, Lupin) and complex generics are the export upgrade.

GCC link

Indian manufacturers supply a large and rising share of generics and biosimilars to Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the wider GCC; the gates are SFDA/MOHAP registration, GMP inspection, serialisation (RSD, Tatmeen) and a local agent. Localisation incentives in Saudi Arabia and the UAE are pulling Indian firms into JVs and local plants.

Market dynamics

Growth drivers

  • Chronic/lifestyle therapies (anti-diabetic, cardiac, urology, GLP-1 anti-obesity) driving ~8% domestic value growth; Mounjaro became #1 brand in Dec 2025
  • Pharma PLI: Rs 40,890 crore invested vs Rs 17,275 crore target; cumulative sales Rs 3.17 lakh crore incl. Rs 2.04 lakh crore exports (to Sep 2025)
  • Exports to 190+ countries; US 34% and Europe 19% of exports; Pharmexcil target USD 65 bn exports by 2030
  • Jan Aushadhi (PMBJP): 18,646+ kendras, 2,110 medicines, sales Rs 2,022 crore FY25 expanding affordable generics access
  • Biopharma SHAKTI (Rs 10,000 crore, 5 yrs) and PRIP research scheme supporting innovation and biosimilars

Restraints

  • Domestic volume growth only ~0.5% (MAT Nov-25); growth is price- and new-launch-led
  • US tariff uncertainty in 2025 pulled forward ~USD 1.6 bn of US purchases, distorting FY26 export trend
  • West Asia conflict (Mar 2026) doubled freight and added USD 4,000-8,000 per-shipment surcharges; Pharmexcil estimates Rs 2,500-5,000 crore export loss risk
  • Dependence on imported APIs/KSMs (bulk drug PLI only avoided Rs 3,591 crore of imports so far)

Opportunities

  • Patent expiries: branded generics launches from Mar 2026 at 20-35% of innovator price expected to drive 2-5x unit surges
  • Bulk drug parks (AP, Gujarat, HP; Rs 6,307 crore project cost) and 55,100 MT/yr capacity for 26 critical APIs under bulk-drug PLI
  • GCC/Middle East exports growing (USD 1.32 bn FY21 to USD 1.75 bn FY25) with India-UAE CEPA and Arab Health participation (65 Indian companies, 2025)
  • Complex generics, biosimilars, vaccines and specialty drugs cited by Pharmexcil as next export growth engines
  • Industry capex of ~Rs 45,000 crore planned for FY26 (ICRA)

Challenges

  • Quality compliance (US FDA / revised Schedule M) across ~10,500 manufacturing units and 3,000+ companies
  • Price control (NPPA/DPCO) limiting value growth in essential medicines
  • Rising freight and geopolitical disruption on Gulf and Red Sea routes
  • Innovator-led premium segments (GLP-1s) still dominated by MNC brands

  • Hospital leaders (India): molecule- and class-level tenders, NLEM-aware pricing and a validated savings tracker typically take 8–12% off drug spend — see class-level lots.
  • Manufacturers: GCC registration and serialisation readiness open the highest-margin export markets within reach; our Network carries GCC hospital demand.
