Class, alternatives and procurement notes

Angiotensin receptor blocker (ARB) and ARNI · Cardiovascular · ATC C09CA03.

Same-class alternatives registered in the UAE: Losartan · Telmisartan · Olmesartan · Candesartan · Irbesartan · Azilsartan · Sacubitril-valsartan (ARNI).

Cross-class alternatives: ACE inhibitors.

Procurement note: NDMA recall history (2018).

Usual forms: 40/80/160/320 mg; with HCTZ/amlodipine. UAE/GCC brands we see in tenders: Diovan, Co-Diovan, Exforge, Valsartan (various).
