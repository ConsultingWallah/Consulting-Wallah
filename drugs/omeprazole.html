Class, alternatives and procurement notes

Proton pump inhibitor · Gastroenterology · ATC A02BC01.

Same-class alternatives registered in the UAE: Pantoprazole · Esomeprazole · Rabeprazole · Lansoprazole · Dexlansoprazole · Vonoprazan (P-CAB).

Cross-class alternatives: H2 blockers; Antacids.

Procurement note: PPIs are class-interchangeable for most formularies — a single class-level lot saves 20–40%.

Usual forms: 10/20/40 mg cap; 40 mg inj. UAE/GCC brands we see in tenders: Losec, Risek, Omeprazole (various).
