Class, alternatives and procurement notes

Non-opioid analgesic / antipyretic · Pain & anaesthesia · ATC N02BE01. Interchangeable for fever/mild pain; paracetamol is the formulary default.

Same-class alternatives registered in the UAE: Metamizole (dipyrone) · Aspirin (analgesic dose).

Cross-class alternatives: NSAIDs (different risk profile).

Procurement note: Highest-count analgesic; IV bag is the cost driver — benchmark per gram and restrict IV to NPO patients.

Usual forms: 500/650 mg tab; 125–250 mg/5 ml syrup; 1 g/100 ml IV; 80–500 mg supp. UAE/GCC brands we see in tenders: Panadol, Adol, Fevadol, Perfalgan (IV).
