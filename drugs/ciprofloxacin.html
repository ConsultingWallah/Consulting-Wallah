Class, alternatives and procurement notes

Fluoroquinolone · Anti-infectives · ATC J01MA02. Class-interchangeable for most indications; restrict to one oral + one IV.

Same-class alternatives registered in the UAE: Levofloxacin · Moxifloxacin · Ofloxacin · Norfloxacin · Delafloxacin · Prulifloxacin.

Cross-class alternatives: Co-trimoxazole; Nitrofurantoin (UTI); Macrolides (respiratory).

Procurement note: Reserve-list in many stewardship programmes; FDA boxed warnings.

Usual forms: 250/500/750 mg tab; 200 mg/100 ml, 400 mg/200 ml IV; 0.3% eye/ear drops. UAE/GCC brands we see in tenders: Ciprobay, Ciprolet, Ciprofloxacin (various).
