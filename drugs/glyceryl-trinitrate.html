Class, alternatives and procurement notes

Antianginal and heart failure · Cardiovascular · ATC C01DA02.

Same-class alternatives registered in the UAE: Isosorbide dinitrate / mononitrate · Nicorandil · Ranolazine · Trimetazidine · Ivabradine · Digoxin · Vericiguat · Levosimendan · Milrinone.

Procurement note: Crash cart (SL/spray) + ICU infusion.

Usual forms: 0.5 mg SL tab; 400 mcg spray; 2.6/6.4 mg SR; 5 mg/ml inj; 0.2% ointment (fissure). UAE/GCC brands we see in tenders: Nitrolingual, Nitromint, Glytrin, GTN (various), Rectogesic.
