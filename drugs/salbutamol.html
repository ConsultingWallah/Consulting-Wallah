Class, alternatives and procurement notes

Short-acting bronchodilator (inhaled) · Respiratory · ATC R03AC02.

Same-class alternatives registered in the UAE: Levosalbutamol (levalbuterol) · Terbutaline · Ipratropium · Salbutamol-ipratropium.

Procurement note: Nebules: high-volume paediatric/ED line; MDI with spacer preferred for cost and outcomes.

Usual forms: 100 mcg MDI (200 doses); 2.5/5 mg nebule (respule); 2/4 mg tab; 2 mg/5 ml syrup; 0.5% solution. UAE/GCC brands we see in tenders: Ventolin, Salbutamol (various), Airomir.
